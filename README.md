# MaskMirror

> Privacy-first identity infrastructure exploring the idea of Web7: ownership of identity exposure.

## Vision

The internet gave us usernames.

Cryptography gave us digital ownership.

Mask Mirror explores the next step:

**Ownership of identity exposure.**

People should control not only who they are, but also:

* what they reveal,
* when they reveal it,
* to whom they reveal it,
* and for how long.

---

## Core Principle

```text
Real Identity = Ownership
Alias Identity = Usage
Proof = Trust
```

---

## The Idea

Mask Mirror separates identity into three layers:

```text
Root Identity
    ↓
Alias Identity
    ↓
Cryptographic Proof
```

### Root Identity

Your legal and verified identity.

### Alias Identity

Your public identity for daily interactions.

### Cryptographic Proof

Prove facts without exposing identity.

Examples:

* Age ≥ 18
* Citizen status valid
* Qualification verified
* Account ownership verified

## Why This Matters

Humans have contextual identities.

We behave differently:

* with family,
* at work,
* in communities,
* online,
* and in public life.

Technology often collapses these contexts into a single permanent identity.

Mask Mirror explores whether individuals should regain control over identity exposure and context.
